Fabrio is an opinionated framework built on [Rialto](https://rialto.buijs.dev/), the
cross-language contract for a whole application. Rialto says what a contract means; Fabrio makes one
choice for every open question and ships the libraries and the generator for it.

Fabrio JVM is being rebuilt in slices, one Rialto profile at a time: the generator reads the
contract into an IR, a pass per profile adds its meaning, and emitters write the files. The
[status per slice](https://github.com/buijs-dev/fabrio-jvm#status) is in the README.
